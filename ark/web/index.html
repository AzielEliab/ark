    About

    The ARK is a local vault by Aziel Eliab. Files stay in a folder on this computer. This page does not send them anywhere.

    The phrase is the login. A different phrase opens a different vault. A forgotten phrase leaves those files unreadable.

    Not a kernel, not a bootable OS. “Rotating Kernel” means the rotating crypto engine. The intake check looks at files you choose to store.
